# 5/3/1 Strength Program (4-Day)

## Overview
Jim Wendler's 5/3/1 method, built around four main lifts trained once per week each, using
percentages of a "training max" (90% of true 1-rep max) across a 3-week wave before a deload.

## Training Max
Training Max (TM) = 90% of your true 1-rep max for Squat, Bench Press, Deadlift, and Overhead
Press. All working percentages below are relative to the TM, not the true max.

## Weekly Split
- Day 1: Overhead Press (main lift) + upper accessories
- Day 2: Deadlift (main lift) + posterior chain accessories
- Day 3: Bench Press (main lift) + upper accessories
- Day 4: Squat (main lift) + leg accessories

## Wave Structure (repeats every 3 weeks, then deload)
- Week 1 ("5s week"): 65% x5, 75% x5, 85% x5+ (AMRAP on last set)
- Week 2 ("3s week"): 70% x3, 80% x3, 90% x3+ (AMRAP on last set)
- Week 3 ("5/3/1 week"): 75% x5, 85% x3, 95% x1+ (AMRAP on last set)
- Week 4 (deload): 40% x5, 50% x5, 60% x5, no AMRAP

## Accessory Work (per day, after main lift)
- 5x10 of a supplemental lift (e.g., Squat day: Leg Press 5x10)
- 3 sets of a pulling movement for balance (e.g., Barbell Row or Pull-Up)
- 2-3 sets of direct core work

## Progression Rule
After each 4-week wave (3 work weeks + deload), add 5 lb to the Training Max for upper body
lifts (Press, Bench) and 10 lb for lower body lifts (Squat, Deadlift).

## Notes
Requires only a barbell, plates, and a rack — no machines strictly needed, making it usable in
a well-equipped home gym or commercial gym.
