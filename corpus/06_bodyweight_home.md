# Bodyweight-Only Home Program (3-Day)

## Overview
A no-equipment, full-body program for travel, small apartments, or anyone without gym access.
Uses tempo and unilateral variations to add difficulty as bodyweight reps get easy.

## Workout A
- Push-Up (or Incline/Knee Push-Up if needed) — 4 sets x max reps (leave 2 in reserve)
- Bodyweight Squat / Bulgarian Split Squat — 4 sets x 12-15 reps
- Inverted Row (table or low bar) / Doorway Row — 4 sets x 10-12 reps
- Plank — 3 sets x 30-60 sec
- Pike Push-Up (shoulders) — 3 sets x 8-12 reps

## Workout B
- Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift (bodyweight) — 3 sets x 10 reps/leg
- Decline Push-Up (feet elevated) — 3 sets x 10-15 reps
- Glute Bridge / Single-Leg Glute Bridge — 3 sets x 15 reps
- Superman Hold — 3 sets x 20-30 sec
- Side Plank — 3 sets x 30 sec/side

## Equipment Note
Zero equipment required. A pull-up bar, resistance band, or backpack of books are optional
additions that unlock harder progressions but are not mandatory.

## Progression Rule
Progress difficulty (not load) — move from knee push-ups to full push-ups to decline push-ups;
from bodyweight squats to Bulgarian split squats to single-leg squats — once the current
variation is easy for all prescribed reps with 2+ reps in reserve.

## Notes
Best for travel weeks, home workouts, or as a deload option between gym-based blocks.
