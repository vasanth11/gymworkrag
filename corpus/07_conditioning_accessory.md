# Conditioning & Accessory Add-On Day

## Overview
A single optional add-on day meant to be layered onto any of the other programs (PPL, 5/3/1,
Upper/Lower, Full-Body, Hypertrophy Block, Bodyweight) for lifters who want extra conditioning
or weak-point accessory work without adding a full heavy training day.

## Conditioning Block (pick one)
- 20-minute incline treadmill walk, Zone 2 heart rate
- 10 rounds: 30 sec Assault Bike sprint / 90 sec easy pace
- 3 sets: 400m row or run at moderate pace, rest 2 min between

## Accessory Block (pick 3-4)
- Face Pull — 3 sets x 15-20 reps
- Band Pull-Apart — 3 sets x 20 reps
- Copenhagen Plank — 2 sets x 20-30 sec/side
- Farmer's Carry — 3 sets x 40m
- Cable Crunch — 3 sets x 15 reps
- Wrist Curl / Reverse Wrist Curl — 2 sets x 15 reps

## Progression Rule
Conditioning: extend duration or reduce rest interval by 10% every 2 weeks. Accessories: add
one set every 2 weeks up to a max of 4 sets, then increase load.

## Notes
Equipment scales to whatever is available (treadmill, bike, rower, or none — the accessory
block can be done with just a resistance band). Not a standalone program; intended as an
add-on to the day-of-week programs in this library.
