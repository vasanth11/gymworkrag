# Hypertrophy Specialization Block (5-Day, Bodypart Split)

## Overview
A high-volume bodybuilding-style bodypart split for advanced lifters who prioritize muscle
growth over raw strength. Each muscle group gets one dedicated, high-volume day per week.

## Day 1: Chest
- Flat Barbell Bench Press — 4 sets x 8-10 reps
- Incline Dumbbell Press — 4 sets x 10-12 reps
- Cable Fly — 3 sets x 12-15 reps
- Dips (weighted if possible) — 3 sets x 10-12 reps

## Day 2: Back
- Weighted Pull-Up — 4 sets x 8-10 reps
- Barbell Row — 4 sets x 8-10 reps
- Seated Cable Row — 3 sets x 10-12 reps
- Straight-Arm Pulldown — 3 sets x 12-15 reps

## Day 3: Legs
- Back Squat — 4 sets x 8-10 reps
- Leg Press — 4 sets x 10-12 reps
- Leg Extension — 3 sets x 15 reps
- Seated Leg Curl — 3 sets x 12-15 reps
- Standing Calf Raise — 4 sets x 12-15 reps

## Day 4: Shoulders & Arms
- Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press — 4 sets x 8-10 reps
- Cable Lateral Raise — 4 sets x 12-15 reps
- Barbell Curl — 3 sets x 10-12 reps
- Rope Triceps Pushdown — 3 sets x 10-12 reps

## Day 5: Weak-Point / Accessory Day
- Chosen based on lagging muscle group (e.g., rear delts, calves, biceps) — 4-5 exercises,
  3 sets x 12-20 reps each, moderate rest (45-60 sec)

## Progression Rule
Track total weekly volume (sets x reps x load) per muscle group; increase volume ~5-10% every
2 weeks, deload every 5th-6th week by cutting volume in half for one week.

## Notes
Requires a full commercial gym with cable stations. Not recommended for lifters with under 1
year of consistent training due to the recovery demand.
